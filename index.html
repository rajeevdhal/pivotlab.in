Our approach

A process built for founders.

Every transaction is different. But the discipline behind a great outcome is consistent — rigorous preparation, a wide buyer universe, and relentless focus on value.

01Diagnostic & Positioning

We start by understanding your business deeply — financials, growth drivers, competitive moat, and the story that will resonate with the right buyers. We identify gaps and close them before going to market.

Business auditValue driversNarrative craftingFinancial normalisation

02Buyer Universe & Outreach

We map the full universe of strategic and financial acquirers — domestic and cross-border. Our outreach is targeted, confidential and designed to generate competitive tension without noise.

Strategic buyersPE & growth fundsCross-border acquirersConfidential outreach

03Process Management

We run a structured, time-bound process — managing NDAs, information flow, management presentations and indicative offers. We protect your time and keep the business running while the deal moves forward.

NDA managementData roomManagement presentationsIOI & LOI negotiation

04Negotiation & Close

We negotiate hard on your behalf — on price, structure, earnouts, representations and warranties. We coordinate with legal and financial advisors to get to a clean, certain close.

Valuation negotiationDeal structuringEarnout designClosing coordination
